ATTACHMENT ADVISOR

Build fewer attachments that do more

Quick-swap · repeatable · mission-specific

The strongest attachment is not always the most complicated one. Nature Nexus should prefer rigid guides, hard stops and self-aligning shapes that make the robot land in the same place every time. The AI groups missions that can share one mechanism.

1

Universal quick-swap mount

Use one consistent pin/axle interface on the front or top of the robot so attachments slide on in one motion. Avoid rebuilding between runs.

2

Passive before powered

If a wedge, fork, guide or hook can solve the mission without another motor, it is usually faster and more reliable.

3

Self-aligning geometry

Add funnels, side rails and physical stops. Let LEGO geometry correct small navigation errors instead of relying only on perfect code.

4

Design for reset speed

Every attachment should have an obvious home position and be installable correctly even when the technicians are under match pressure.

EFFICIENCY RULE

One owner, one backup, one checklist

For every run, assign one primary technician and one backup. The primary owns robot placement and launch; the backup owns the correct attachment, mission pieces and next-run setup. During practice, a data recorder tracks success rate so the AI reliability sliders reflect real results.

SMART COMBINATIONS

Mission synergies the AI watches

M02 → M14

Seed chain

Collect the three Exploding Seeds instead of scattering them, then reuse them for Seeds of Renewal. The M09 seed can also contribute.

M05 + M07

Root + fungus

A fully extended root can support the cooperative M07 connection bonus. Coordinate with the opposing team before the match.

M08 + M09

Shared tree trip

Tangled and Research Platform are built into the same major model, so a well-designed tree attachment can target several scoring actions in one visit.

M13–M15

Dock strategy

These interchangeable models use the docks. Choose placement based on your robot's easiest approach and the M15 environmental bonus.

M10

Score by doing no harm

Fragile Microhabitats rewards leaving both habitats in their starting positions. Route planning matters as much as an attachment.

Precision

Reliability is points

Six or five precision tokens are worth 50 points. A slightly lower mission score can beat an aggressive plan that causes repeated interruptions.

OFFICIAL UPDATE CHECK

Important Oct. 14, 2026 changes

UPDATE 03

Tree stabilization · M08 & M09

Events are encouraged to stabilize the tree with a thin strip of black tape behind it. Practice with both taped and untaped versions.

UPDATE 02

Interchangeable docks · M13–M15

The robot may autonomously remove an interchangeable model from its dock using the model's built-in latch.

UPDATE 01

Lucky Leaves · M04

Leaf positions are randomized and teams are not told the katydid's starting placement. Build a solution that tolerates variation instead of relying on one exact setup.
