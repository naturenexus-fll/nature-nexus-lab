TOOL & ATTACHMENT ADVISOR

What can each role build or add?

Rule-aware ideas
Rule boundary: Future Edition allows non-electric LEGO bricks in any quantity, but electronic hardware is limited to the exact permitted types/quantities. The Driver base can receive attachments when it is completely inside the Technician Area. Operator tools are mechanical/non-electric and must stay in the Operator Area. The Technician robotic tool uses one single motor + one color sensor. The Specialist primarily controls the Grand Tree and programs. Controller grips/guards are allowed but cannot perform other game actions.

PRACTICE METHOD

Train primary + backup skills before choosing match roles

Players cannot switch roles once a match begins. During practice, rotate everyone through all four roles, record success rates, then assign match roles based on evidence: calm driving, accurate launching, fast attachment work, coding confidence and field awareness.

HIGH-VALUE COMBINATIONS

Future Edition synergies

M01 + M04

Release → place keystones

Free keystone species from the Nest, then route them toward microbiomes. The Young Forest fourth keystone can also trigger the opposite-side invasive queen bonus.

M02 + Specialist

Resource pipeline

Operator feeds resources into the Grand Tree base while the Specialist cycles them upward to the canopy chamber. Smooth rhythm matters more than random speed.

M03 + Tree rotation

Waterfall engine

The Specialist reveals the Cave Waterfall entrance, then the team repeatedly cycles keystone species through it. The mission can reward sustained throughput.

M05

Shared endgame

Both teams receive the full shared Central Haven score. Coordinate before and during the match so late deliveries do not block each other.

Level Up

Risk-for-points choice

Add only as many invasive species as the team can realistically contain. More added pieces raise scoring potential but also increase field congestion and execution risk.

Driver + Technician

Mobile tool carrier

The driving base may move the Technician robotic tool outside the Technician Area, opening combined strategies for collection, launching and delivery.
